Privacy Policy — Study Clock

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 • Package Name: com.salman.studyclock • Developer: Salman Mallah (developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com)

1. Overview & Offline-First Design

Study Clock (com.salman.studyclock) is 100% free with all features unlocked — including all Pure OLED themes, clock fonts, Focus Card templates, and CSV study export — and is built offline-first. You can use all core clock, flip clock, countdown timer, stopwatch, Pomodoro, and local study session tracking features in Guest Mode without creating an account or syncing your study logs to our servers. Study Clock is supported by minimal, non-intrusive Google AdMob ads shown only outside active or paused focus sessions (subject to UMP consent where required), which involves standard ad-delivery and Advertising ID data collection by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as detailed in Section 4 below.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Study & Work Session Logs: Stored locally in an on-device SQLite database (study_tracker.db) including subject/tag name, category (Study/Work), session start/end timestamps, and duration.
  • App Preferences: Clock format (12h/24h), orientation preference, alarm tone URI, theme/font selections, and profile display settings (display name and short bio) stored in Android SharedPreferences protected by Android file-based encryption and android:allowBackup="false".

3. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Firestore Sync

If you choose to sign in using Sign in with Google (via Android Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication), Study Clock collects and syncs the following data to your private account in Google Cloud Firestore:

  • Account Identifiers, Profile & Preferences: Firebase User ID (UID), display name, email address, short bio, custom subject tags (customTags), daily goal minutes (dailyGoalMinutes), tracker mode (trackerMode), and sync timestamps (updatedAtMillis, prefsUpdatedAtMillis) stored under users/{uid}.
  • Study Session Backup: Your study/work session records stored under users/{uid}/study_sessions/{sessionId} so your progress stays backed up and synchronized across your devices.

We never sell your personal profile or study logs to third-party data brokers.

4. Advertising, Advertising ID & UMP GDPR Consent

  • Google AdMob & SDK Data Collection: Study Clock shows minimal, focus-safe ads strictly outside focus sessions: adaptive banner ads appear only in Settings and Profile (no banner ads are placed on Clock, Timers, or Study Dashboard) and are automatically hidden whenever any Study, Timer, Stopwatch, or Pomodoro session is running or paused. Frequency-capped transition interstitial ads (shown only when exiting Settings) are strictly capped at 2 per day with a 15-minute cooldown, zero cold-start ads, and are never shown during active or paused focus sessions. To serve and measure ads and prevent invalid traffic/fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google: device Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), IP address (which may be used by Google to estimate coarse location), app interaction/ad impression events, and device diagnostic information (such as device model, OS version, screen dimensions, and ad performance diagnostics).
  • EU/EEA/UK User Consent (UMP SDK): For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Study Clock uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent before initializing ads. Users can review, change, or revoke their ad personalization consent at any time via Settings → Support & Legal → Privacy Options.

5. Device Permissions Explained

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for optional Cloud Firestore sync, Google Sign-In, and AdMob ad loading.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ for ad delivery, frequency capping, and invalid-traffic protection.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used on Android 13+ to display active study session chronometer notifications and countdown timer completion alerts.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Keeps your active study session chronometer notification running accurately when you minimize the app during a study session.
  • USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Ensures countdown timers and Pomodoro alerts ring at the exact second they finish.
  • VIBRATE: Used for tactile haptic feedback and countdown timer / Pomodoro completion alerts.
  • DETECT_SCREEN_CAPTURE (Android 14+): Detects when you take a screenshot inside the app solely to offer an optional on-screen shortcut to the Focus Share Card Studio. No screenshots are ever uploaded or transmitted off your device.

6. Data Retention Schedule & Account / Data Deletion

Retention Periods & Criteria:

  • Local On-Device Data (SQLite & SharedPreferences): Retained locally on your device only for as long as Study Clock remains installed, or until you clear app storage, uninstall the app, or trigger in-app account deletion.
  • Synced Cloud Account, Profile & Study Sessions (users/{uid} and users/{uid}/study_sessions/*): Retained in Google Cloud Firestore for as long as your Study Clock account remains active so you can sync and restore your study history across devices. We do not auto-expire active study logs, because users rely on multi-year career and semester study analytics; instead, these records are retained until you delete them in-app or submit a deletion request.
  • Google AdMob Telemetry: Any ad-serving and Advertising ID signals collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are retained and governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

How to Delete Your Account & Data:

  • In-App Instant Deletion: Open Study Clock → Settings → Account Card (Profile) → Delete Account & Data. After Google re-authentication, this immediately deletes your synced study/work sessions (users/{uid}/study_sessions/*), deletes your Firestore profile document (users/{uid}), wipes local device account logs, deletes your Firebase Authentication user record, and signs you out to Guest Mode.
  • Out-of-App Web Deletion Request: Visit https://salmanmallah.github.io/delete-account.html or email developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com from your registered Google Account email address. Out-of-app deletion requests are fulfilled within 7 calendar days.
