Privacy Policy — Study Clock

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Package Name: com.salman.studyclock • Developer: Salman Mallah (developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com)

1. Overview & Offline-First Design

Study Clock (com.salman.studyclock) is built offline-first. You can use all core clock, flip clock, countdown timer, stopwatch, Pomodoro, and local study session tracking features in Guest Mode without creating an account or transmitting personal data to any server.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Study & Work Session Logs: Stored locally in an on-device SQLite database (study_tracker.db) including subject/tag name, category (Study/Work), session start/end timestamps, and duration.
  • App Preferences: Clock format (12h/24h), orientation preference, alarm tone URI, theme/font selections, and profile display settings stored in Android SharedPreferences protected by Android file-based encryption and android:allowBackup="false".

3. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Firestore Sync

If you choose to sign in using Sign in with Google (via Android Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication), Study Clock processes and syncs the following data to your private account in Google Cloud Firestore:

  • Account Identifiers & Profile: Firebase User ID (UID), display name, email address, short bio, avatar preset or custom avatar thumbnail, and Google profile photo URL stored under users/{uid}.
  • Study Session Backup: Your study/work session records and custom subject tags stored under users/{uid}/study_sessions/{sessionId} so your progress stays backed up and synchronized across your devices.

We never sell or share your personal profile or study logs with third-party data brokers.

4. Google Play Billing & Lifetime Premium Verification

Study Clock offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (premium_lifetime) processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details. When you purchase or restore Lifetime Premium while signed in, our backend Firebase Cloud Function (verifyPremiumPurchase) verifies the purchase token with the Google Play Developer API and stores an authoritative entitlement record under users/{uid}/entitlements/premium and a SHA-256 hashed token record under purchase_tokens/{tokenHash} to prevent replay fraud and enable cross-device purchase restoration.

5. Advertising, Advertising ID & UMP GDPR Consent (Free Tier Only)

  • Google AdMob: Free-tier users may see non-intrusive banner ads (in Settings, Profile, or Study dashboard) and frequency-capped transition interstitial ads (never shown during active study, timer, stopwatch, or Pomodoro sessions). Verified Lifetime Premium users never initialize the Google Mobile Ads SDK and see zero ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ for ad serving, frequency capping, analytics, and invalid-traffic/fraud prevention in accordance with your device settings and consent choices.
  • EU/EEA/UK User Consent (UMP SDK): For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Study Clock uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent before initializing ads. Free-tier users can review, change, or revoke their ad personalization consent at any time via Settings → Support & Legal → Privacy Options.

6. Device Permissions Explained

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for optional Cloud Firestore sync, Google Sign-In, Play Billing verification, and AdMob ad loading.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used on Android 13+ to display active study session chronometer notifications and countdown timer completion alerts.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Keeps your active study session chronometer notification running accurately when you minimize the app during a study session.
  • USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Ensures countdown timers and Pomodoro alerts ring at the exact second they finish.
  • DETECT_SCREEN_CAPTURE (Android 14+): Detects when you take a screenshot inside the app solely to offer an optional on-screen shortcut to the Focus Share Card Studio. No screenshots are ever uploaded or transmitted off your device.

7. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

You can delete your Study Clock account and associated data at any time using either of two methods:

  • In-App Instant Deletion: Open Study Clock → Settings → Account Card (Profile) → Delete Account & Data. This immediately deletes your synced study/work sessions (users/{uid}/study_sessions/*), deletes your Firestore profile document (users/{uid}), deletes your Firebase Authentication user record, wipes local device account logs, and signs you out to Guest Mode.
  • Web Deletion Request (Without the App): Visit https://salmanmallah.github.io/delete-account.html or email developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com. Web deletion requests are processed within 7 calendar days.
  • Retained Purchase Entitlement Record: If you purchased Lifetime Premium, the non-PII entitlement record (users/{uid}/entitlements/premium and hashed purchase token purchase_tokens/{tokenHash}) is retained solely for Google Play purchase restoration and fraud prevention. You may request complete erasure of this record by contacting developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com.
