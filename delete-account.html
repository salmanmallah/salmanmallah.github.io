Account & Cloud Data Deletion — Study Clock

Application: Study Clock (com.salman.studyclock) • Developer: Salman Mallah (developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com)

Option 1: Instant In-App Account & Data Deletion (Recommended)

If you have Study Clock installed on your Android device, you can delete your account and cloud data immediately inside the app:

  1. Open Study Clock and go to Settings.
  2. Tap your Account Card at the top to open the Profile screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Tap Delete in the confirmation dialog and confirm the Google account re-authentication prompt.

Your Cloud Firestore profile (users/{uid}), synced study/work sessions (users/{uid}/study_sessions/*), custom tags, local device logs, and Firebase Authentication user account are deleted immediately and the app returns to Guest Mode.

Option 2: Out-of-App Account Deletion Request (Via Email or Webmail)

If you have uninstalled Study Clock or cannot access the app, you can request account and cloud data deletion without installing the app by emailing developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com from the Google Account email address linked to your Study Clock account. To protect your account from unauthorized third-party deletion, we verify ownership by checking that the email originates from your registered Google Account address and, if additional confirmation is required, replying to that address for a one-time confirmation before completing deletion within 7 calendar days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained & Retention Schedule

  • Cloud Account & Server Data (Deleted Immediately In-App / Within 7 Calendar Days via Email Request): Firebase Authentication account record (UID, email address, display name), Cloud Firestore user profile document (users/{uid}), all synced study/work session records (users/{uid}/study_sessions/*), and synced custom subject tags.
  • Local On-Device Data (SQLite & SharedPreferences): Deleted immediately when you use Option 1 (In-App Deletion), or when you uninstall Study Clock / clear app storage in Android Settings. (Because local SQLite files reside strictly on your physical device, an out-of-app email request deletes your server-side cloud account and cloud backups, while uninstalling the app removes local device files.)
  • Zero Server-Side Retention After Deletion: No personal account or study log data is retained on our servers after deletion completes.
