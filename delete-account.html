Account & Cloud Data Deletion — Study Clock

Application: Study Clock (com.salman.studyclock) • Developer: Salman Mallah (developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com)

Option 1: Instant In-App Account & Data Deletion (Recommended)

If you have Study Clock installed on your Android device, you can delete your account and cloud data immediately inside the app:

  1. Open Study Clock and go to Settings.
  2. Tap your Account Card at the top to open the Profile screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Tap Delete in the confirmation dialog (if prompted by Google for security verification, confirm your Google account).

Your Cloud Firestore profile, synced study/work sessions, custom tags, Firebase Auth user record, and local device logs are deleted immediately and the app returns to Guest Mode.

Option 2: Submit a Web Account Deletion Request (No App Required)

If you have uninstalled Study Clock or cannot access the app, submit a deletion request below or email developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com from the Google Account email associated with your Study Clock account. Web requests are processed within 7 calendar days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted: Firebase Authentication account record (UID, email address, display name, photo URL), Cloud Firestore user profile document (users/{uid}), all synced study/work session records (users/{uid}/study_sessions/*), custom subject tags, and local SQLite session history.
  • Retained for Purchase Restoration & Fraud Prevention: If you purchased the one-time Lifetime Premium upgrade (premium_lifetime), the non-PII entitlement verification record (users/{uid}/entitlements/premium and SHA-256 purchase token hash purchase_tokens/{tokenHash}) is retained solely so Google Play Billing can restore your purchase if you reinstall the app and prevent duplicate token fraud. You may request complete erasure of this entitlement record by selecting "Complete Erasure" above or emailing developer.salmanmallah@gmail.com.
